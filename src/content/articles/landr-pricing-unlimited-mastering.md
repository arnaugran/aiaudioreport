LANDR sells "unlimited AI mastering" and that is true in the same way an all-you-can-eat
buffet is unlimited if you only want the bread. On the entry plan, unlimited means unlimited
MP3s. There is no WAV master on it at all.

That one line changes which plan you need, so it is worth going through the tiers properly.

<div class="takeaways">
<h2>The short version</h2>

- Studio Essentials, €12/month, lists unlimited MP3 masters and no WAV masters.
- Studio Standard, €18/month, adds three WAV masters a month. Three.
- Studio Pro, €23/month, is the only tier with unlimited WAV.
- If you need more than three lossless masters a month, Pro is not the upsell, it is the entry point.
- Yearly pricing was mid-sale when we checked. Read the struck-through numbers, not the headline.

</div>

## The plans, as published

Read on 8 November 2026. LANDR showed us euros.

<div class="tablewrap">

| Monthly | Price | Masters | Distribution | Sample credits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Studio Essentials | €12/mo | Unlimited MP3 | 1 artist, 5 to 7 day review | 100/mo |
| Studio Standard | €18/mo | Unlimited MP3 + **3 WAV/mo** | 3 artists, 2 to 5 day review | 150/mo |
| Studio Pro | €23/mo | **Unlimited WAV & HDWAV** | 5 artists, 2 to 5 day review | 200/mo |

</div>

The mastering column is the whole story. Everything else scales the way you would expect, and
the format of the file you get out does not.

An MP3 is lossy. It is fine for a reference, a demo, a rough upload to share with a
collaborator. It is not the file you keep as your master. So on Essentials you are paying €12
a month for an unlimited supply of files you would not archive, and on Standard you are
paying €18 for three real ones.

Standard works out at €6.00 per WAV master if you use all three. Pro is already cheaper per
master at four, and Standard physically cannot reach four. So the decision is not "is Pro
worth the extra €5" but "do I need a fourth lossless master this month". If yes, there is one
plan.

If you master one or two tracks a month, Standard is the right call and the €6 each is
reasonable for what it is. If you are working through an album, Pro pays for itself almost
immediately.

## The yearly prices need a second look

When we checked, a Black Friday sale was running, which makes the yearly column harder to
read than it should be.

<div class="tablewrap">

| Yearly | Sale price | Struck-through list price | Badge |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Studio Essentials | €7.42/mo billed yearly | none shown | 5 months free |
| Studio Standard | €7.48/mo billed yearly | €10.83/mo | 31% off, 7 months free |
| Studio Pro | €11.25/mo billed yearly | €22.50/mo | 50% off, 6 months free |

</div>

Two things worth noticing before the sale ends.

The "7 months free" style badges compare the yearly rate to the monthly rate, not to the
yearly list price. They move when the sale moves.

And the list yearly prices are not consistent between tiers. Standard's €10.83 a month billed
yearly is a genuine discount against its €18 monthly. Pro's €22.50 sits a whisker under its
€23 monthly, which at list price is a rounding error rather than a reason to commit for a
year. During a sale that reverses completely. Outside one, check which tier you are actually
on before paying twelve months up front.

Yearly sample credits match twelve months of the monthly allowance exactly, at 1,200, 1,800
and 2,400. No hidden bonus there in either direction.

## What we are not telling you

We have not compared this to what a human mastering engineer charges. We could not find a
primary, current rate card we were willing to quote, and inventing a "typical" figure to make
a comparison look rigorous is exactly the thing this site exists not to do. That comparison
is on the list for when we can source it properly.

We have also not assessed how LANDR's masters sound. We do not run listening tests. For that
judgement, find someone who does and who says what they listened on.

## Who should buy what

**One or two tracks a month, and you want lossless.** Studio Standard. Three WAV masters is
enough and €6 a master is fair.

**An album, an EP, or any month with four or more tracks.** Studio Pro. The cap on Standard
decides this for you long before the price does.

**You only need references and demo uploads.** Essentials, and know that you are buying MP3s.
If a lossless file ever becomes necessary, the plan you are on does not produce one.

**You are mostly after the plugins and sample credits.** Price those separately before
subscribing. The plugin bundles are quoted at large notional values, and a "€2,000 value"
bundle is worth what you would otherwise have spent, which for most people is not €2,000.
