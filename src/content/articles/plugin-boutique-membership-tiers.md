Plugin Boutique membership is free. Silver and Gold unlock automatically when your spending over the previous year passes the store's thresholds. Gold is the only tier that improves the cash-back rate, from 5% to 7.5%, and adds a monthly free plugin.

The perks look better on paper than they are for everyone. Here's what I think is worth counting, and what I'd ignore unless you already shop there.

## The three tiers

| Tier | How you get it | Virtual Cash | Monthly free plugin | Partner perks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Member | Create an account | 5% | No | No |
| Silver | €90+ spent in 12 months | 5% | No | Yes |
| Gold | €180+ spent in 12 months | 7.5% | Yes | Yes |

Every tier includes a free gift with each purchase, a free plugin bundle and the Plugin
Boutique desktop app. Existing customers were enrolled automatically, so if you have bought
anything there in the past year you already hold a tier.

One wrinkle on the thresholds. The membership page quotes €90 and €180. Plugin Boutique's own
explainer article gives the same two tiers as £75 and £150, which is the same scheme shown in
a different store currency rather than a different offer. That explainer also still contains
a literal `[CONFIRM]` placeholder sitting next to the Silver figure, left in the published
page, which is a reasonable prompt to check the threshold against your own account rather
than against either article.

## Silver is the odd one out

Reaching Silver costs you €90 of spending and does not improve your cash back at all. It stays
at 5%, exactly the free tier's rate. What you get is the partner discount list.

Whether that is worth anything depends entirely on whether you already wanted those things.
The list is long and genuinely varied, covering ear training subscriptions, studio furniture,
headphones, distribution, remote collaboration tools and music courses. Several entries are
free trials rather than discounts. If two of them match something already on your list, Silver
pays for itself without you spending a penny more. If none do, Silver is a label.

## Gold, and what 2.5 points is actually worth

Gold is the only tier that moves the rate, from 5% to 7.5%. Put in money rather than
percentages, that is **€2.50 extra for every €100 you spend**. On the €180 that qualifies you,
the gap between the two rates is €4.50.

That is not nothing, but it is not the reason to chase Gold either. The monthly mystery plugin
is. In November the Gold gift was listed as the JUNO-60 CHORUS by Roland, with 22 days left to
claim. One plugin a month, every month, is worth considerably more than four euros fifty, as
long as you actually want what shows up, which you cannot know in advance.

Plugin Boutique does not say whether the higher rate applies retroactively to the spending
that qualified you, and I won't assume it either way.

## The Discovery Bundle has a monthly penalty

There is a second route to Gold. The Discovery Bundle is a paid subscription, and subscribing
grants Gold rewards. Its pricing is where this gets interesting.

The annual plan is £150 a year. The monthly plan is £15 a month, which is £180 across twelve
months. So paying monthly costs £30 more, and on top of that the explainer states plainly that
"Monthly subscribers earn 5% Virtual Cash" rather than Gold's 7.5%.

Paying monthly for the same product is therefore worse twice over. That is unusual enough to
be worth saying out loud, because the normal pattern is that monthly costs a little more and
is otherwise identical.

## Virtual Cash is store credit, and it spends like cash

All of the above rests on what Virtual Cash actually is, and the help centre answers it
plainly even though the Terms and Conditions never mention it.

> With every transaction, you will earn 5% back in Virtual Cash that you can apply at the
> checkout.

The useful part is what comes next. You can "apply any amount that you have available in your
Virtual Wallet", and Plugin Boutique is explicit that it stacks: "You can even save up enough
Virtual Cash to get some plugins free of charge, using only what you have earned."

So there is no per-order cap and no minimum, and a balance can cover a whole product. That
makes 5% back behave very close to a 5% discount deferred to a later order, and 7.5% to a
7.5% one. The balance sits in the Rewards section of your Library, under the wallet icon, and
also shows at checkout.

That is worth knowing because loyalty currencies usually are not this simple. Loopcloud
points, for example, cost two per loop rather than one, so the headline number and the real
one differ. Here they do not.

## What I'd do

Do nothing. Membership is free, automatic, and you are already in it.

Do not spend to reach a tier. Spending €180 to earn €4.50 more in cash back is a bad trade,
and the partner perks only pay off if you already wanted two of them.

Treat Gold as a side effect. If you buy plugins regularly you will pass €180 without trying,
and the monthly free plugin is a genuine benefit once you are there. If you do not, chasing it
is the tail wagging the dog.

If you are considering the Discovery Bundle, pay annually or not at all.

Worth knowing for context: Plugin Boutique Limited is part of the Beatport Group, the same
company whose [AI policy I read for a separate piece](/articles/can-you-upload-ai-music/).
